Who We Are

Our Mission

UCSRN is the national student representative body for university college students across the Netherlands.

01

We Represent

We advocate for the interests of university college students at national and European level, lobbying for greater recognition of the merits of Liberal Arts and Sciences education.

02

We Unite

We bridge the gaps between institutions, enabling the creation of a nationwide network of ambitious, diverse, and broadly-educated students across all 11 UCs.

03

We Support

We are a centralised platform for sharing ideas and best practices, providing financial support for inter-UC academic events, social events, and student initiatives.

What Drives Us

Brand Values

Three core values define everything we do and how we show up for our students.

🔗

Connection

We are the bridge between UCs across the Netherlands — a platform where students come together to exchange ideas, share experiences, and build lasting inter-UC friendships.

🌍

Inclusivity

We believe in diversity as a key to enabling critical discussions. We welcome students of all socio-economic backgrounds, cultures, races, nationalities, and genders.

🚀

Growth

We move forward together as a community — continuously learning, supporting each other, and taking Liberal Arts and Sciences education in the Netherlands to new heights.

How We Work

Our Structure

UCSRN operates through a lean, student-led governance structure.

Executive Board

The Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, and Commissioner of External Affairs form the Executive Board, elected annually at the General Assembly by member associations.

Advisory Body

Alumni and external experts who guide strategic decisions, ensure institutional memory, and provide continuity across board transitions.

Academic Committee

Organises inter-UC academic events, conferences, and workshops promoting intellectual exchange and Liberal Arts & Sciences education.

Social Committee

Coordinates inter-UC social events, sports leagues, and Spotlight — fostering connection and community across all member colleges.

Get Involved

Join the Board

Shape the future of university college education in the Netherlands. Board elections are held annually at the General Assembly.

1

Check Eligibility

You must be a current student at one of the 11 UCSRN member University Colleges and able to commit to the full academic year.

2

Attend an Info Session

Join one of our online or in-person info sessions where current board members explain the roles, time commitment, and answer your questions.

3

Submit Your Application

Send your motivation letter and CV to [email protected] before the deadline.

4

Interview Round

Shortlisted candidates are invited for a short interview with the outgoing board and Advisory Body members.

5

Election at General Assembly

Final candidates are presented at the GA, where member associations vote. Results are announced at the end of the Assembly.
